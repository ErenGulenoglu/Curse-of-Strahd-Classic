**Stanimir** is a Chaotic Neutral **mage** [[Vistani]] who is tasked by [[Madam Eva]] to bring adventurers to [[Barovia]] to save [[Strahd von Zarovich]] from his curse. He is the elder of his travelling Vistani band. He has two children, [[Damia]] and [[Ratka]]. He is a good storyteller.
