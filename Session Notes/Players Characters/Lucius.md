**Lucius** was raised by a hag in the wilderness, stolen away from his human mother as a baby. Transformed into a hexblood by his hag ‘mother,’ he spent his life learning magicks so that he may one day transform into a hag and become her successor. While Lucius loved his hag ‘mother’ and was happy to become like her, he could not help but yearn to see the outside world: Lucius was forbidden by the hag from ever leaving her lair, a dark, cold cave deep within a mountain. Why it was forbidden, he did not know - and the hag refused to say.

For all his life, Lucius obeyed this rule unquestionably. But as he matured, and as he remained restricted to the cave, his dark home began to feel more and more like a prison, and his hag mother its cruel warden. No matter his pleas, the hag would never let him leave; no matter his efforts or improvements in the ways of magic, she always had a new excuse. The resentment within Lucius stirred and grew until one day, while the hag was distracted, he took the opportunity to run down the maze of caverns and finally, finally escape. The moment Lucius stepped into the daylight is one he will never forget: feeling the warmth of the sun on his skin for the very first time, seeing the world filled with vibrant color, he realized all that the hag had taken from him when she stole him all those decades ago. He fled down the mountain, promising to himself to never return to the hag and filled with a new desire to find his mysterious human mother.

For years Lucius wandered the isolating wilds as a hermit, taking the time to bask in the sunlight and explore the bright, colorful world that had been kept from him. It is during this time that he grew to revere and worship the Sun, revering it for its power to illuminate the world, feed its creatures, and melt the chill that had grown within him. Lucius spent years studying and communing with the Sun as ancient magickers do, forming a deep connection to the ancient star that soon allowed him to harness a portion of the Sun’s fire and gave his purpose: to spread its warm light into the deepest, darkest corners of the world.

His pilgrimage to spread the Sun’s light would eventually lead Lucius to wander into the town of Daggerford, where he learned of learn of civilization and met other people for the first time. The villagers (understandably) were not kind to the hag-spawn zealot at first, seeing him and his worship as sacrilege. It was not until Lucius joined up with other locals to form an adventuring party and defeated some local threats that he was finally (but warily) allowed within Daggerford as a respected local hero - though many villagers still avoid and fear him. Even so, Lucius is not deterred, and in fact continues to journey with his adventuring party in spite of the hatred he receives. To him, spreading the Sun’s light is all that matters - and perhaps if he defeats enough evil through its fire, the world will come around to his worship and see him as more than a hag-spawn. But he’s not holding his breath.
