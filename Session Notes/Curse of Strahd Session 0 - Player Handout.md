**Core Player Information**
- Your characters are a part of small adventuring party that reside in [[Daggerford]]. You serve under [[Duchess Morwen]] of Daggerford.
- You are local heroes, and friends that have been working together for a time. Maybe you have fought against bandits, some undead that stirred from their grave, or a troll that harassed the caravans that followed High Road of northern Sword Coast.
- After living in Daggerford for years—maybe since your birth—as a group you decided to travel to towards south, following High Road, maybe to Baldur's Gate to seek the excitement and beauty of adventuring life elsewhere.
- You will be leaving Daggerford in couple of days during the Midsummer feast. During this time you help the town to prepare for the Midsummer feast, hanging flags, painting buildings, placing flower pots to streets, arranging festival spaces, and so on.
- And, in your last night, before you leave, you are invited for one last dinner with Duchess Morwen of Daggerford.
- You will be Level 3 when starting this adventure. Lucky Feat is banned.

**Optional Information**
- _Curse of Stahd_, is a campaign that is experienced going in blindly. So, I will not be trying to cultivate your character creation as much as I did with _Without A Sound_. People who are expecting a very character focused story will be disappointed because the world in this campaign... is a bit different.
- If you want to integrate lore of Forgotten Realms to your characters' backstories you are welcome to.
- If you want to be involved in Factions of Forgotten Realms, such as Harpers, Zhentarim, and so on, please do so. Factions can give unexpected colors to your characters!
